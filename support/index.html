Frequently asked questions

Why does Roomtally say 10 boxes when I count 9?

There are two ways to turn an area into boxes, and they can differ by one. By the box label: the area plus waste, divided by the coverage printed on the box, rounded up once. By tiles per box: the tiles you need, rounded up, divided by the tiles in a box. For a 12′ 6″ × 10′ 3″ floor with 12 × 24 in tiles and 10 % waste, a box labelled 15.6 sq ft gives 10 boxes; 8 tiles per box gives 9. The label is the default because it already allows for the joints. “Why N” under the result names the method it used, and when the two numbers you entered disagree, Roomtally shows both instead of choosing one quietly.

How are waste and rounding worked out?

Waste is a percentage added to the net area or volume before anything is rounded: 10 % for a straight lay, 15 % for diagonal, 20 % for a pattern, 10 % for wallpaper and concrete, 5 % for gravel, mulch and soil. Roomtally then divides by what one box, can, roll, sheet or bag covers and rounds up once, at the end. Rounding in between (tiles first, then boxes) can leave you short, so it never does. The waste line and the spare are always shown, for example “9 boxes · 71 tiles incl. 10 % waste · 1 spare”.

On the shopping list, the same item from several rooms is added up before rounding: 4.4 + 4.4 boxes make 9 boxes, not 10. One tap shows how much each room needs.

Why is wallpaper counted in drops, not by area?

Wallpaper is hung in drops cut from a roll, and a pattern repeat makes every drop longer. Roomtally rounds the wall height up to whole repeats, works out how many drops one roll gives, counts the drops around the room and adds 10 %. Wall area divided by roll area ignores the offcuts and can come up short: with a 0.64 m repeat, 7 rolls by area become 9 by drops. In the US the single roll is only the unit prices are quoted in; you buy double rolls.

Can I use feet and inches, or metric?

Each project keeps its own units: feet, inches and fractions down to 1/16″ (or 1/8 to 1/32), or millimetres and metres. While you type, the size is echoed in the other system, for example “= 12.531 ft · 3 820 mm · rounded to 1/16″”. Sizes are stored exactly, so nothing drifts when you switch. You can type 12′ 6 3/8″, 3.82 m or 3820 mm, paste a size copied from Measure, or skip the sizes with “I know the area”.

What is free, and what does Roomtally Unlock add?

Free, for good: one project of up to three rooms with every material, the full working with “Why N”, the shopping list with departments and ticks, history, sharing the list as text, the widgets and the Control. The one-time Roomtally Unlock adds unlimited projects and rooms, export of the list as PDF and CSV, and alternate app icons. There is no subscription and no advertising, and nothing that is free today will be locked later. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought the unlock with, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings. The purchase belongs to that Apple Account, so it works on every iPhone and iPad signed in with it.

A number looks wrong. What can I do?

Every preset says where it was read and when, or that it is an assumption to check against your label. Change any preset and it is marked “yours”: new calculations use your value, existing ones keep theirs. If a preset itself is wrong, tap Report a wrong number on the Numbers tab: the email names the preset, your region and the app and Numbers versions. Every preset with its date and source is listed in the table below.

Does the stair screen check my stair against the building rules?

No. It works out the number of risers, the riser height and the going from your floor-to-floor height, within the ranges of a country preset, and says so: “Within the … preset range · guidance only”. Each preset names the edition it was read from, such as IRC 2021, Approved Document K 2013 or DIN 18065:2020-08, and its source is in the table below, together with the editions that have not been read yet. Building rules differ by region and change over time; only your local building authority can say whether a stair is acceptable.

How do I add the widgets and the Control?

Touch and hold the Home Screen or the Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose Roomtally: List Progress shows how much of the list is in the cart and what is next, Last Result the latest calculation. In Control Center, add New calculation to start measuring from anywhere. Siri and Shortcuts can work out Tile boxes for a room size, paint for a room and your shopping list.

Are my projects synced or backed up?

Your projects are stored only on your iPhone, in storage the app shares with its own widgets on the same device. Roomtally has no server and doesn't sync between devices. Projects are part of your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), so they move to a new iPhone set up from a backup. To keep a copy elsewhere, share the list as text, PDF or CSV.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a room on the Rooms tab and tap Delete; its sizes and materials go, and its rows leave the list. A finished project can be archived from the project menu on the List tab. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone, the widgets' included. We hold no copy, because nothing is ever sent to us.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Roomtally runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Dutch, Polish and Russian. It works fully offline.

Every number and where it comes from

A preset is the number a new calculation starts with: how far a gallon or a litre of paint goes, what a bag of concrete yields, how much extra to buy. Each one names where it was read and when; an assumption is a common size that was not checked against a label. Change any of them and it is yours: new calculations use it, existing ones keep their own.
